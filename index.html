    Principles behind the appWhy the protocol prioritizes fewer, higher-value actions.

    Less Is More

    Fewer supplements, fewer tests, fewer habits — each one earning its place across multiple health domains.

    Outcomes Over Mechanisms

    Evidence grades come from human outcome data — meta-analyses and RCTs — not from marketing words like "NAD+" or "autophagy".

    Redundant Means Skip

    If your diet already covers it, the supplement gets a "probably redundant — skip" label. Your money stays yours.

    Carnivore-Honest

    Iron, B12 and zinc are covered — and iron is genuinely dangerous to add. What carnivores lack is stated plainly, with solutions.
