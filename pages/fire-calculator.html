⚠️ Important: This calculator uses simplified assumptions. The 4% rule (FIRE Number = 25x annual expenses) is based on US historical data. Singapore's higher healthcare costs and CPF LIFE payouts mean your actual number may differ. CPF LIFE payouts from age 65 onwards can significantly reduce the portfolio you need — aim for your investments to last from retirement age to 65, then CPF LIFE takes over. The CPF LIFE estimate is a rough heuristic — actual payouts depend on your retirement sum, chosen plan (Standard/Escalating/Basic), and payout start age. Use the CPF payout estimator at cpf.gov.sg for accurate figures. This is not financial advice. Consult a licensed adviser.
