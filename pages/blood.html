    Singapore screening

    Use the Healthier SG eligibility check for your age, last test and health history. These are typical starting ages and intervals for eligible people. Ask your clinic about earlier assessment if your risk is higher.

    • Cervical screening from 25. For eligible women, a Pap test every three years at 25–29 or an HPV test every five years from 30.
    • Cardiovascular risk screening from 40. Typically every three years for eligible adults. Higher-risk adults aged 18–39 may qualify through the Diabetes Risk Assessment.
    • Colorectal screening from 50. Typically one FIT stool test each year for eligible adults.
    • Breast screening from 50. Typically a mammogram every two years for eligible women.

    Bring previous results and ask whether add-ons such as ApoB or Lp(a) would change your care. If you already have high blood pressure, diabetes or high cholesterol, review your treatment and targets with your clinic.
